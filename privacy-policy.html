Privacy Policy

Mune  ·  Last updated: September 10, 2026

Mune is a personal wellness app designed to help you track your mood, journal your thoughts, and receive gentle AI-powered reflections. Your privacy is fundamental to how this app was built.

1. What Data We Collect

Stored on your device (stays local unless you use an AI feature below):

  • Your name or nickname
  • Profile photo
  • Mood check-ins and any notes you write
  • Journal entries and voice transcriptions
  • App preferences (healing focus, reminder time, display settings)
  • No-contact streak settings and calm-space drawings you choose to save

Sent when you use AI features (mood reflections or Talk to Mune):

  • Your nickname
  • Mood labels and counts from recent check-ins
  • Optional check-in notes you wrote
  • Chat messages you type
  • Brief recent journal context (short journal snippets, transcript highlights, or gratitudes) when chat is personalized

Requests go through a Cloudflare Worker proxy we operate, which forwards them to OpenAI over HTTPS. The proxy exists so an API key is not embedded in the app. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

3. What We Never Collect

  • Precise location
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Analytics or third-party ad tracking
  • Cloud accounts or passwords (Mune is local-first)

4. Third-Party Services

Cloudflare Workers is used as a secure proxy for AI requests. We do not operate a general Mune user database.

OpenAI, LLC powers AI reflection and chat. You can opt out by not using Chat or AI reflections.

Apple frameworks used on device include SwiftData, UserNotifications, the system Contacts picker, PhotosUI, and Speech (on-device recognition when available).

You can revoke any permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security on your iPhone.

6. Important health notice

Mune is a breakup support and wellness tool. It is not therapy, not medical care, and not a crisis service. If you or someone you know is in crisis:

  • Find a local helpline: IASP Find a Helpline (worldwide)
  • Emergency services: call 112 in the EU (including Germany), or your local emergency number (for example 911 in the US)
  • United States: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)

7. Children's Privacy

Mune is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Deletion

All data stored on your device can be deleted by leaving or deleting a local profile space, or by deleting the app. We do not keep a cloud copy of your journal.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
