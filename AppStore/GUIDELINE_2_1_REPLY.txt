2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
================================================================================
Mune is a private companion for the days after a breakup. It is for adults and
older teens (rated 13+) who have recently ended a relationship and are dealing
with the hours that hit hardest: 2am, an empty evening, or the moment they are
about to contact an ex they have decided not to contact.

The problem it solves: in that moment a person wants to act immediately, and
acting usually makes the next day worse. Most apps in this space count the days
since last contact and reward a streak, which turns a hard night into a number
the person can fail.

Mune's value is that it intervenes in the moment instead of measuring it. One
tap opens a guided pause that walks the person through the urge, lets them write
the message they will not send, and hands back one small next step. Around that
sit a two-minute urge-surfing timer paced by breathing, a structured thought
challenge, small daily rebuilding commitments, and a self-rated recovery trend.

Mune is support, not therapy, medical care, or a crisis service, and it says so
in the app, in the Chat header, and in the Terms of Use.

================================================================================
3. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================
No credentials are required. There is no demo account because there are no
accounts of any kind. Nothing is gated, region-locked, or behind a purchase.

To set up: launch the app, tap "Get started", type any nickname, optionally pick
a healing focus, and continue. Everything is then available.

To reach the main features:
- Calm Space tab, top button "I want to text them" - the guided urge pause.
- Calm Space tab, "2 quiet minutes", then "Start the 2 minutes" - timed
  urge-surfing exercise paced by breathing.
- Calm Space tab, also contains paced breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a drawing
  pad, call-a-friend through the system contact picker, and crisis links.
- Journal tab, prompt row, "Reality Check" - name a thought, test it, write a
  fairer answer. The same tab has writing prompts and "Speak it out" dictation.
- Home tab, check-in section - pick one or more feelings, optionally add a note,
  and save to receive an insight.
- Home tab, scroll down to "Rebuild yourself" - small daily commitments across
  body, mind, and people.
- Home tab, "Recovery progress" - self-ratings for pain, intrusive thoughts,
  contact urge, sleep, loneliness, and sense of self, shown as a trend.
- Home tab, "Healing days" - the optional day counter, off by default.
- Chat tab - breakup-aware support. Starter chips are provided, for example
  "I want to text them".
- Settings, "Your data" - export or delete everything stored on the device.
- Profile - change nickname, photo, and healing focus.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS, AND PLATFORMS
================================================================================
- OpenAI (chat completions API): generates the replies in the Chat tab and the
  short insight shown after a check-in. This is the only third-party service
  that receives user text.
- Cloudflare Workers: hosts our own proxy at
  https://mune-openai-proxy.shehani1207.workers.dev. The proxy holds the OpenAI
  credential so no API key ships in the app. It forwards the request and returns
  the reply. We do not operate any other backend and we store nothing.
- Apple frameworks only, for everything else: SwiftUI and SwiftData (local
  storage), Speech (dictation, configured for on-device recognition so audio
  never leaves the phone), Contacts (the system contact picker for call-a-friend),
  MessageUI (the user's own Mail composer for optional feedback), AVFoundation
  (audio playback), and UserNotifications (an optional local daily reminder).
- Pixabay: the Calm Space soundscape is a bundled audio asset licensed under the
  Pixabay Content License, which permits commercial use in apps. This is an
  asset, not a runtime service. Provenance is documented in
  THIRD_PARTY_AUDIO.md in our repository.
- GitHub Pages: hosts our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use web pages only.

There is no authentication provider, no payment processor, no advertising
network, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no tracking identifiers. Mune
contains no third-party source code or app template; it is written by hand in
SwiftUI and SwiftData.

================================================================================
5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
================================================================================
All features work identically in every region. Nothing is geo-restricted, and
no content differs by country except the crisis resources, which are localised
deliberately for user safety:

- The local emergency number is chosen from the device region. We only show a
  number for regions where we are confident of it (North America and much of the
  Caribbean, UK, EU and EEA, Australia and New Zealand, India, Japan, Korea,
  China/Hong Kong/Macau, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, the Gulf states, and
  several others).
- Where we are not confident of the local number, we deliberately show no number
  and instead link to the IASP worldwide helpline directory, so a user is never
  given a wrong emergency number.
- In the United States we additionally surface the 988 Suicide and Crisis
  Lifeline for both call and text.

One further environmental difference, not regional policy: the "Speak it out"
dictation in Journal depends on iOS on-device speech recognition being available
for the device language. Where it is not, the feature reports that it is
unavailable and the person types instead.

================================================================================
6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND PROTECTED THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
================================================================================
Mune does not operate in a regulated industry. It is a wellness and lifestyle
app. It is not a medical device, does not offer diagnosis or treatment, does not
provide therapy or counselling by a licensed professional, and is not a crisis
service. It reads no HealthKit data and holds no clinical records. These limits
are stated in the app, in the Chat header ("Not therapy"), in Settings, and in
the Terms of Use at https://shehanish.github.io/Mune/terms-of-use.html.

Where distress signals appear in what a person writes, Mune does not attempt to
treat anything. It surfaces official helpline links, including the IASP
directory and, in the United States, the 988 Lifeline. That text is deliberately
never forwarded to AI.

The only protected third-party material in the app is the Calm Space audio,
licensed from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License, which expressly permits
commercial use in applications and requires no attribution. Documentation of the
source, the original filename, and the licence is kept in THIRD_PARTY_AUDIO.md
and can be supplied on request. All other assets, illustrations, written
content, and source code are our own original work.
