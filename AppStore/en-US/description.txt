Mune is a private breakup companion for the moments that feel unbearable — when you want to send the text, check their profile, or start the day already heavy.

RIDE THIS WAVE
A two-minute pause when the urge to reach out hits. Breathe through it. Write it here if you need to. Nothing is saved or sent.

CALM SPACE
Guided breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a private vent pad, drawing, and a way to call someone you trust. Crisis links are here if a friend is not enough.

TALK WITH MUNE
Breakup-aware support for grief, missing someone, and getting through the hard hours. Chat stays in memory. It is not stored in the app.

JOURNAL YOUR WAY
Write or record by voice. Use gentle prompts, including a letter you will not send. Your words stay on your iPhone.

CHECK IN WITH YOURSELF
Log how you feel. See a quiet picture of your week. Optionally keep healing days as a reminder you are caring for yourself — not as a streak to protect.

PRIVATE ON PURPOSE
No account. No feed. No ads. Mood, journal, and profile data stay on your device. AI features go through our proxy to OpenAI only when you use them.

Mune offers support, not therapy. If you are in crisis, find a local helpline or call emergency services.
