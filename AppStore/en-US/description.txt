Mune is a private breakup companion for the moments that feel unbearable: when you want to send the text, check their profile, or start the day already heavy.

WHEN YOU WANT TO TEXT THEM
One tap slows the moment down. Sit through a short pause, look at the thought again, and decide with a clearer head. Nothing here is saved or sent.

TWO QUIET MINUTES
A short timer for when the feeling spikes. Stay with it until it softens. No decisions to make, nothing saved, nothing sent.

CALM SPACE
Guided breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a drawing pad, and a way to call someone you trust. Crisis links are here if a friend is not enough.

TALK WITH MUNE
Breakup-aware support for grief, missing someone, and getting through the hard hours.

JOURNAL YOUR WAY
Write or record by voice. Gentle prompts for when you do not know where to start, including a letter you will not send.

CHECK IN WITH YOURSELF
Log how you feel and see a quiet picture of your week. If it helps, count healing days since your last contact as care for yourself, not as a streak to protect.

REBUILD, ONE SMALL STEP
Pick one small thing for your body, your mind, or the people around you. Mune keeps the days you showed up, not the days you missed.

YOURS TO KEEP OR DELETE
No account, no feed, no ads. Journal entries, check-ins, rebuild steps, and your profile stay on this device. Chat messages and check-in insights are sent to AI through our proxy only when you use those features. You can export everything as a text file, or delete your space whenever you want.

Mune offers support, not therapy. If you are in crisis, find a local helpline or call your local emergency number.
