WHAT MUNE IS
A private companion for the days after a breakup, built around one moment: the urge to contact an ex. Mune intervenes in that moment rather than counting days. Support, not therapy.

No account, email, or password. Tap Get started, type any nickname. No in-app purchases or paywall, so nothing is gated.

WHERE TO LOOK (two minutes)
1. Calm Space > "I want to text them". Guided pause: name the urge, write the message you will not send, get a next step. Nothing saved or sent.
2. Calm Space > "2 quiet minutes". Timed urge-surfing paced by breathing.
3. Journal > prompt row > "Reality Check". Name a thought, test it, write a fairer answer.
4. Home > "Rebuild yourself". Daily steps for body, mind, and people. Counts days shown up, not days missed.
5. Home > "Recovery progress". Self-ratings for pain, intrusive thoughts, urge, sleep, loneliness, sense of self.
6. Chat > chip "I want to text them". Redirects to an exercise, not to replaying the relationship.

Calm Space also has breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a drawing pad, call-a-friend, and worldwide crisis links. Crisis text is never sent to AI.

PRIVACY
Journal, check-ins, rebuild steps, reality checks, snapshots, and drawings stay on the device. Chat text, optional check-in context, and the nickname go through our Cloudflare Worker to OpenAI to generate replies, declared as Name and Other User Content for App Functionality, never tracking. Users export or delete everything in Settings. No analytics, ads, or tracking identifiers. Calm Space audio is licensed from Pixabay.
