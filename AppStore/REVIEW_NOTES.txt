WHAT MUNE IS
Mune is a private companion for the days after a breakup. It is built around a single moment: the urge to contact an ex. Rather than counting days of abstinence, Mune intervenes in that moment. It slows the decision down, gives the person something to do with the feeling, and hands them one small step for the day after. It is support, not therapy, and the app says so wherever it matters.

NO SIGN-IN NEEDED
There is no account, no email, and no password. Tap Get started, type any nickname, and every feature is immediately available. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription, and no paywall, so nothing is gated during review.

HOW TO SEE WHAT MAKES MUNE DIFFERENT (about two minutes)
1. Calm Space tab, top button "I want to text them". A guided pause: name the urge, write the message you will not send, then receive a next step. Nothing is saved or transmitted.
2. Calm Space tab, "2 quiet minutes", then "Start the 2 minutes". A timed urge-surfing exercise paced by breathing. No decisions, nothing saved.
3. Journal tab, prompt row, "Reality Check". A structured thought challenge: name the thought, test it against evidence, write a fairer answer.
4. Home tab, scroll to "Rebuild yourself". Small daily commitments across body, mind, and people. It counts the days the person showed up instead of punishing the days they missed.
5. Home tab, "Recovery progress". Periodic self-ratings for pain, intrusive thoughts, contact urge, sleep, loneliness, and sense of self, shown as a trend.
6. Chat tab, starter chip "I want to text them". Breakup-aware support that redirects toward an exercise rather than replaying the relationship.

Also in Calm Space: paced breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a drawing pad, and call-a-friend through the system contact picker. Crisis links are available worldwide.

Crisis-signal detection surfaces helplines on Home, in Journal, and in Chat, and deliberately does not forward that text to AI.

PRIVACY AND DATA
- No account, no analytics SDK, no ads, and no tracking identifiers.
- Stays on the device: journal entries, check-ins, rebuild steps, reality checks, recovery snapshots, drawings, and the profile.
- Leaves the device: chat messages, optional check-in context, and the nickname, sent through our own Cloudflare Worker to OpenAI to generate a reply. Declared as Name and Other User Content, linked to identity, App Functionality only, never used for tracking.
- The person can export or delete everything from inside the app (Settings, then Your data).
- The bundled Calm Space audio is licensed from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License, which permits commercial use in apps.

HEALING DAYS IS NOT THE POINT
The optional healing-days counter is off by default, framed as care rather than a streak, and is deliberately neither the lead feature nor the first screenshot.
