APP REVIEW NOTES
On our account history: an earlier submission under the working title Mend: Heal Your Heart was rejected under 4.3(a). We did not contest that decision. The title created confusion with an unrelated App Store app of a similar name, which was our mistake. We withdrew that record and renamed the app to Mune, with a new bundle identifier and a rewritten listing.

We are not affiliated with Novra Development LLC or Mend - No Contact Tracker, and we do not use an app template or third-party source code. Mune is written by hand in SwiftUI and SwiftData.

Since that review we rebuilt the app around a guided intervention loop rather than a day counter. These features have no counterpart in the app we were compared to:

- "I want to text them": a guided pause that walks you through the urge, lets you write the message you will not send, and hands you a next step instead of a streak
- Two quiet minutes: a timed urge-surfing exercise with breathing pacing. Nothing is saved or sent
- Reality Check: a structured thought-challenge tool. You name the thought, test it, and write a fairer answer
- Rebuild: small daily commitments across body, mind, and people, tracking the days you showed up rather than punishing the days you missed
- Recovery Progress: periodic self-ratings for pain, intrusive thoughts, contact urge, sleep, loneliness, and sense of self, shown as a trend
- Calm Space: paced breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a drawing pad, call-a-friend through the system contact picker, and worldwide crisis links
- Crisis-signal detection that surfaces helplines on Home, in Journal, and in Chat, and deliberately does not forward that text to AI

Mune also differs commercially and on privacy: no account, no subscription or in-app purchases, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking identifiers. Journal entries, check-ins, rebuild steps, reality checks, and recovery snapshots stay in on-device storage, and the user can export or delete all of it from within the app.

Healing days is optional, off by default, framed as care rather than a streak, and is deliberately not the lead feature or the first screenshot. Please review Mune on that basis.
